A program that writes a one-sentence absurd clause buried deep inside a terms-of-service agreement to `/output/entry.txt` for make.fun's "The Absurd Clause Contest" bounty.

The winning line:

> In the event of a dispute, the parties agree to settle the matter by interpretive dance, judged by three pigeons selected by the Company at its sole discretion.

A dispute-resolution clause in perfect legal register, right up until the tribunal turns out to be three pigeons judging an interpretive dance.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
